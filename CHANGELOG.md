## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10
### Added
- Floating shelves: shake the pointer while dragging, press ⌃⌥D, or drop onto the menu bar icon or MacBook notch
- Liquid Glass shelf with a stacked pile, hover fan-out, and a detail list (sizes, resolutions, durations)
- Drag everything out at once; reorder rows; inline rename; Quick Look; dock shelves to screen edges
- Instant Actions (Messages, AirDrop, Mail, Compress) and built-in actions: share, ZIP, convert/resize images, extract text (OCR), Shortcuts, custom scripts
- Suggested actions: stitch images, images → PDF, merge PDFs, video → GIF, compress video
- Command Bar (⌘K) to search every action
- Clipboard history shelf (⌃⌥V) that skips password-manager content
- Watched folders and automatic screenshot collection
- Drop sound and haptic with a sound picker and volume
